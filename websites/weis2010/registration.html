The Ninth Workshop on the Economics of Information Security (WEIS 2010)

Harvard University, USA

June 7-8, 2010

Conference Registration

Registration Fee
Academic/government/post-doc $400
Student $200
Industry $600

Registration includes admission to all sessions, conference materials, a digital copy of the pre-proceedings, lunch each day of the conference, a reception on Sunday evening, and dinner on Monday night.

Click here to register for WEIS 2010

Participants who also wish to attend the 11th ACM Conference on Electronic Commerce, held June 9-11 at Harvard, can receive a $50 discount each for registering for both conferences. You must register separately for both conferences, but the discount will be reflected in the registration fee paid to both conferences.

Student Scholarships - We hope to offer a number of limited student scholarships covering the registration fees and/or stays at the conference hotel based on double occupancy. Please email weis2010@econinfosec.org with subject 'WEIS student scholarship' by May 5.

Note: receipts will be available at the conference.

Registration for the workshop is open to the public. If you have any questions, please contact weis2010@econinfosec.org.

Members of the press are invited to attend the workshop. Please email weis2010@econinfosec.org if you would like media credentials.

WEIS is co-located with the 11th ACM Conference on Electronic Commerce, June 9-11.
