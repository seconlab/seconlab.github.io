STUDENT SCHOLARSHIPS

Student scholarships will be awarded for up to $750 per student to attend the workshop. This funding is provided by the Institute for Information Infrastructure Protection (I3P) to defray the costs of attendance, which include registration, travel, hotel, and workshop events. Students should have a strong background and interest in fields related to the economics of information security and privacy, including computer science, engineering, law, economics, and the social sciences.

The application deadline was April 21, 2008.

To apply, please send an email including the following information:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Home address
  • Academic institution
  • Program and major
  • Student status (e.g., Master, PhD, Post-Doc) and year
  • Main advisor's name
  • A brief statement (no more than 500 words) about why you are interested in attending the workshop. What research issues are central to your research activities and plans?

Also, send a short letter of recommendation from a faculty member and a recent CV to:
