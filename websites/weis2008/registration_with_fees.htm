REGISTRATION

Registration is open to anyone wishing to participate in the workshop. However, precedence will be given to the presenters of selected papers.

Registration fees include the attendance at workshop sessions, pre-proceedings, continental breakfasts, refreshment breaks, lunches, dinners, and Wednesday night reception, as well as optional regional activities for attendees staying through Saturday, June 28.

Student scholarships are available for qualified students to attend the workshop. The application deadline for scholarships was April 21, 2008. For more information, visit the Scholarships page.

Please note: Registering for the Workshop does not reserve a hotel room. Rooms are being held at the Courtyard by Marriott, Marriott Residence Inn, and the Hanover Inn. The hotel cut-off date for the special Workshop rates is May 21, 2008 for all three hotels. Visit the travel page for more information.

You can register online through the I3P secure server. This system accepts Visa, MasterCard, and American Express credit cards.
