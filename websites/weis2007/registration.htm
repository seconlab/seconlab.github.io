REGISTRATION

Registration is open to anyone wishing to participate in the workshop. However, precedence will be given to the presenters of selected papers.

Registration fees include the attendance at workshop sessions, pre-proceedings, continental breakfasts, refreshment breaks, lunches, and Wednesday and Thursday night reception.

Student Scholarships are available for qualified students to attend the Workshop. The application deadline has been extended to 11:59 pm EST on May 1, 2007. More information.

Please note: Registering for the Workshop does not reserve a hotel room. Rooms are being held at the Holiday Inn Select University Center and Wyndham Garden Hotel at special rates. Hotel cut-off dates for the special Workshop rates are May 16 for the Holiday Inn and May 6 for the Wyndham Garden. Visit the travel page for more information.

You can register online through CMU secure server. This system accepts Visa and MasterCard credit cards. The registration deadline is May 7, 2007.
