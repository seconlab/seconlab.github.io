The organizers are pleased to announce a keynote addess by Ontario's Information and Privacy Commissioner, Dr. Ann Cavoukian.

For a possible preview of the event check the contributors of the text, The Economics of Information Security by Springer/Kluwer. An edited collection of reviewed papers. The work includes work by Camp, Varian, Anderson and Schneier. This book is designed as a text for a graduate seminar or an introductory volume for the interested scholar. Selected papers from the Economics of Information Security Workshop Series - The First and Second Workshops.
