The Fifth Workshop on the Economics of Information Security (WEIS 2006)

Robinson College, University of Cambridge, England

26-28 June 2006

Please note that online registration closes at midnight, Thursday, June 22.

Registration is open to anyone wishing to participate in the workshop, not only those intending to present a paper.

Please note: Finding accommodation in Cambridge this June will be difficult if you do not book soon. Click here for information regarding accommodation.

WEIS is co-located with the Sixth Workshop on Privacy Enhancing Technologies, June 28-30. PET immediately follows the conclusion of WEIS on the afternoon of June 28; WEIS attendees are invited to attend the opening Wednesday session of PET. For those who wish to attend the entire PET workshop, we are pleased to offer a discount for registering for both workshops.
